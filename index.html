Professional work · The most challenging problem I have solved

Finding the feedback that keywords miss

AI orchestration for a survey portal built for eXp, a US real estate company.

Context

The orchestration layer summarised feedback from both Agent and Employee surveys. I managed it.

Problem

A useful summary depends on retrieving the relevant feedback. People often express the same idea in different words, so keyword search alone can miss it.

Solution

I used hybrid search, combining BM25 keyword ranking with vector retrieval on OpenSearch k-NN/HNSW. Using both improves retrieval coverage.

Survey feedbackAgent and Employee surveys
Embeddingstext-embedding-3-small, 1,536 dimensions
OpenSearchText index and k-NN/HNSW vectors
Hybrid retrievalBM25 + vector similarity
SummaryBuilt from relevant feedback

The platform used Node.js, Express, TypeScript, Prisma and PostgreSQL. Its 36 route files followed a controller, service and repository structure. The search engine also clustered up to 2,500 vectors per clustering operation.

Why use both?
